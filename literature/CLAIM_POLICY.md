# Literature and claim policy

The registry prefers primary publications and records the exact relation between
each source and the Atlas scope.

A source marked `context_only` or `method_reference` cannot support a numerical
Atlas record. Importing a result requires a separate claim object with the
original parameterization, page or figure, converted Atlas parameters,
coordinate reconstruction, evidence status, and two-person review.

The initial registry is intentionally small. Search results, secondary
summaries, and adjacent packing problems are never silently converted into
claims about congruent triangles in rectangles.
